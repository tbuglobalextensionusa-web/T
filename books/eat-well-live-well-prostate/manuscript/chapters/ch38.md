### Opening Perspective

Aging is this book's final physiological context — the terrain where every prior chapter's science meets time's irreversible passage, where prevention's discipline confronts decline's reality, and where the difference between merely surviving decades and thriving through them is determined by the same variables this book has championed throughout: knowledge, practice, partnership, and dignity. This chapter does not promise to stop aging (no honest book can); it promises to equip the man for aging intelligently — understanding what changes and why, what deserves intervention and what deserves acceptance, what the evidence supports and what the market oversells, and how identity and meaning evolve alongside physiology so that the decades ahead are lived with purpose rather than endured with dread. The chapter's structure follows the aging-arc: first, the physiology of change across this book's domains with age-adjusted expectations; second, the decade-specific priorities for the fifties, sixties, seventies, and beyond; third, the intervention-landscape's honest tiering; fourth, the identity-and-meaning work; fifth, the care-partnership structures; and sixth, the thriving-program's assembly — the complete-response to the question every man past fifty asks silently: how do I age well?
