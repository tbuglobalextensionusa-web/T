# CHAPTER 34
## Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia Treatments: From Medication to Surgery

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter you should be able to: describe the full BPH treatment ladder — watchful waiting, alpha-blockers, 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors, combination therapy, phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors, and surgical/minimally-invasive procedures — with evidence-based indications for each rung; explain the mechanism of action for each treatment class and how mechanism matching drives selection; apply the urodynamic-evaluation's role in treatment selection for complex presentations; evaluate the surgical landscape (TURP, laser procedures, enucleation, and minimally-invasive therapies) with honest outcome-and-side-effect profiles; navigate the treatment-decision's shared decision-making framework incorporating symptom-burden, prostate-size, and patient-values; and integrate BPH treatment with this book's lifestyle programs and the concurrent management of sexual, urinary, and metabolic health.

### Opening Perspective

Benign prostatic hyperplasia's treatment landscape has expanded from the binary choice of "pills or surgery" into a graduated ladder where every rung's mechanism, evidence, and trade-offs can be matched to the individual man's prostate-size, symptom-burden, values, and concurrent conditions — and this chapter delivers that ladder completely: the watchful-waiting's structured-observation, the pharmacotherapy's mechanism-specific-classes, the minimally-invasive's emerging-options, the surgical's definitive-interventions, and the shared-decision-making's framework that ties them together. The chapter's specific-purpose for this book's audience: the man who has read Chapters 1 through 33 now receives the treatment-execution layer for the condition most likely to intersect his urological life — the-BPH's-treatment-decision-being-this-book's-most-common-clinical-crossroads.

The-integration-with-this-book's-architecture-completes-the-chapter's-context: the-Chapter-3's-BPH-pathophysiology's-treatment-application (the-dihydrotestosterone's-and-stromal-epithelial's-mechanism's-pharmacological-targeting), the-Chapter-31's-storage-symptom's-concurrent-management (the-Chapter-34's-obstruction-ladder's-plus-Chapter-31's-storage-ladder's-coordinated-standard), the-Chapter-30's-incontinence's-overflow-consideration (the-retention's-versus-stress-leak's-differential, the-Chapter-16's-and-17's-sexual-function's-treatment-intersections (the-alpha-blocker's-ejaculatory-effects's-and-5-ARI's-libido-effects's-and-PDE5's-dual-function's-honest-discussion), the-Chapter-32's-pelvic-pain's-concurrent-management (the-obstruction-plus-pain-plus-floor's-combined-contributions), and-the-Chapter-24's-metabolic-program's-and-Chapter-27's-exercise's-lifestyle-support (the-lifestyle's-symptom-modulation-within-treatment-continuum) — making-this-chapter-the-Part-V's-treatment-integration-point.
