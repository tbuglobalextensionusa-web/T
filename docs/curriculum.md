## Additional Integrative Areas

Four questions—Questions 97–100—integrate knowledge across multiple courses rather than introducing separate curriculum subjects.

1. **Integrated clinical assessment:** Managing overlapping anxiety, school refusal, bullying, family conflict, and developmental needs.
2. **Advanced research interpretation:** Investigating differences in intervention outcomes across schools and evaluating implementation factors.
3. **Interprofessional ethical decision-making:** Coordinating counselors, caregivers, and school administrators while protecting adolescent welfare and confidentiality.
4. **Doctoral reflective practice:** Integrating research evidence, clinical expertise, client perspectives, ethics, outcome monitoring, and critical self-evaluation.

## Summary

The 100 MCQs were drawn from 32 listed curriculum courses—24 core courses and eight electives—with four additional integrative questions. The tables above identify the principal knowledge areas represented in the original question bank; they are not an exhaustive syllabus for each course.
