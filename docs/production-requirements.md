# PDF Book Production Requirements

## Project

PhD in Children and Adolescent Counseling.

The curriculum and its course-level coverage map are recorded in [Curriculum](curriculum.md).

## Requirements Supplied by the User

1. **Save the curriculum first.** The supplied coverage description is the starting reference for subsequent educational materials.
2. **Deliver PDF books only.** The user has clarified that voice, audio narration, and other audio deliverables are not required. The earlier request for lecture narration is interpreted as a preference for accessible, conversational lecture-style writing within the PDFs, not as a request for audio production.
3. **Use simple English.** Explain concepts clearly and introduce specialist terminology when needed without sacrificing doctoral-level understanding.
4. **Include real-life applications.** Connect concepts to practical situations involving children, adolescents, families, schools, and counseling services.
5. **Do not avoid areas that require diagrams and illustrations.** Include visual explanations wherever they support understanding.
6. **Each finished book PDF must contain at least 150,000 words.** This is a minimum for each individual book, not a combined word count across the collection. Shorter drafts must not be presented as completed books.

## Production Notes

These notes guide implementation; they do not replace or alter the supplied curriculum.

- Maintain coverage of every listed course and the four integrative areas, while recognizing that the supplied coverage map is not an exhaustive syllabus.
- Write in clear, engaging lecture-style prose, with helpful transitions, worked examples, and step-by-step explanations.
- Include original diagrams, tables, and illustrations where they support understanding. Give each visual a caption and explain its important information in the surrounding text so the lesson does not depend on the image alone.
- Use fictional or appropriately de-identified examples. Do not present invented cases as documented clinical evidence.
- Distinguish research findings, clinical reasoning, ethical requirements, and illustrative examples. Check claims against reliable sources rather than inventing citations or quotations.
- Respect cultural context and avoid treating examples from one setting as universal. Jurisdiction-dependent legal and ethical requirements must be identified as such.
- Present clinical material for education and professional development, not as a substitute for individualized assessment, supervision, or emergency care.
- Produce and review long books in stages, with a course-to-chapter coverage map and a cumulative word-count record. Verify each finished PDF against the 150,000-word minimum before delivery.
- Keep the manuscript sources and accompanying visual assets organized for reliable PDF compilation. Do not generate voice recordings or audio files for this project.

## Confirmed Book Structure

The user has confirmed **one PDF per course**.

- Produce 32 separate course books: 24 core-course books and eight elective-course books.
- Give each book its supplied course code and exact course title.
- Require at least 150,000 words in each finished PDF; the combined collection minimum is therefore 4,800,000 words.
- Follow the order of the supplied curriculum, beginning with CAC.901 and retaining CAC.960 in its listed Year 3 position.
- Incorporate the four integrative areas into relevant course books rather than creating an additional integrative-course PDF.
- Develop and quality-check the collection in stages. Do not describe an outline, sample, or partial manuscript as a finished book.
